# VFX and Visual Identity

## Diagnosing the Problem

The game's visual identity concerning the VFX and spells and magical items around the room is all over the place.
- Almost anything that emits a glow is completely over-exposed and is consumed entirely by the bloom.
- There is almost no visual consistency between VFX.
	- End portal looks like nothing else.
	- Enemy attacks look like nothing else
	- Sigils that appear from enemy attacks look like nothing else
	- Little portal thing above the journal table looks like nothing else
	- Shield looks like nothing else
	- In general, VFX do not match the low fidelity, lack of curves and straight edges of the game's art style.
- 

## Possible Fix

Create basic VFX from scratch.

1. A "rune aura" that emits runes in an upward pattern. They fade in and out over their lifetime.
2. A "rune ring" that is a circular (not an exact circle) of runes. It can spin and expand over its lifetime.
3. A "big rune" that is a single rune. Stays stationary. Will fade in and out over its lifetime.
