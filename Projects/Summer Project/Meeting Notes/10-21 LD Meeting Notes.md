
## Number of Waves Per Level
- I want the player to spend about 40-60 seconds in each room
- Each wave should take about 10 - 20 seconds.
- So, there should be about 3 to 4 waves per-room
- If you feel like you're completing the rooms too quickly, either increase the number of enemies per wave, or increase the number of waves per room

## Number of Enemies per Wave
- NEVER just have 1 enemy in a wave. The enemies aren't strong enough for them to pose a threat individually.
- The number of enemies is heavily dependent on the size of the arena
- 5 - 6 Feels the best in most open areas of the game
- Tighter areas should have 2 to three
