## Updates To The Game

### Cutscenes

#### Basic Cutscenes

These are cutscenes that involve chaining together simple actions to create a fully cohesive sequence of events. These cutscenes will be used for things like entering rooms, interacting with objects, displaying specific dialogue, etc.

#### Sequencer-Based Cutscenes

Fully cinematic cutscenes that are made using Unreal's Sequencer tool. These cutscenes will have more complex animations, camera movements, and visual effects than the basic cutscenes.
