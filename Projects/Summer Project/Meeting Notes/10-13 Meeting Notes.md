# The Game

## Current State of the Game / v0.6.0 Changes

### Dialogue Update
- The dialogue system has been heavily reworked to be a lot less intrusive and more dynamic.
- It should allow us to more naturally present story elements without disrupting the flow of gameplay.
- We just need to fill the game with more dialogue content now.

### Levels
- The materials for the levels have been updated like a lot, so the game looks *very* different now.
- We've also got a couple new levels in the game.

### New Mechanic: Choosing which room to go into
- After clearing a room, the player can now choose to enter a "challenge room" (if they have a "key")
- Challenge rooms have better rewards
- However, they are more difficult and take longer to finish

### New Mechanic: Shield
- The player can now get a shield that absorbs damage
- Shield meter in the top left
