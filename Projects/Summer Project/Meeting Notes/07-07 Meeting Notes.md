### Project Assessment

We took a look at the project in-engine and looked at what we have so far. We discussed some of the major changes:

- Got rid of lumen in favor of baked lighting for the sake of and performance
- Redid some lighting to make it more in-line with the Miro board and intended aesthetic
- Mikel's dialogue portal VFX
- A Souls-like lock-on system has been implemented. Press middle mouse or click in the right stick to toggle lock-on
- We looked at the old UI mockup Alecks did like a month ago and overlaid it onto the game
	- It looked nice, but we need a more polished mockup of all the aspects of the UI first
	- Also, we'll probably have to play around with the colors
- A placeholder health and mana bar that reacts to the player's health and mana
	- If the player dashes, it takes up mana

### What Do People Think?

- Overall, the team is liking the changes.
- Dialogue portal looks cool
- The lighting looks better
- The fog is cool
- However, there is a little bit of contention with the look of the textures for the floor & wall.
	- Should the walls be brighter than the floors or should it be the opposite?
	- Should the flooring texture be more rough or more smoothly tiled?
- The team also thinks the camera should be further out
