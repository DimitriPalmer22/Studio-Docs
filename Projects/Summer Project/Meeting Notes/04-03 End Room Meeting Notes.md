# 04-03 End Room Meeting Notes

## End Room Revamp?

For the end of the game, rather than have some simple room w/ a seal at the end, we can do something a little different.

> Note: this idea revolves around the player reaching the end rooms for the first, second, and third times. However, there is a possibility that the player reaches these rooms more times than that without progressing (due to the quest mechanic.)

### Entrance Room

The starting room.
- Consistent EVERY time the player enters Avernoth
- Serves as a pathway to the special room

#### Concerns

With our journal system, we have gates to the player's progression: If the player has not collected / completed all the journal entries for this "chapter", then they cannot undo the current layer of the seal.

With the existing system we have (a simple seal at the end of the room), we can quickly and very easily tell the player that they cannot progress due to them not having enough journal entries.
- With this new system, how much time do we make the player stay in the room before telling them they cannot continue further?

### Special Rooms

Based on which "Chapter" of Avernoth you are in, the type of special room present after the entrance room will change.

### Special End Room 03 (Twilight)

At the end of each special room lies the seal.

- Big dark room w/ very little visibility
- Also misty (Fog)
- Maze-like room. We'll guide the player through it using landmarking

#### Concerns

What happens when the player didn't collect & complete all the journal entries before coming here?
	- Do they fully navigate the "maze" and then get hit with the realization that they can't progress?

During my 2nd time getting this far, do I have to fully navigate this maze again?

#### Alternate Idea

- When initially getting into the level WITHOUT enough journal entries, the fog is much too thick to see (both visually and in the dialogue). The party acknowledges this and you are forced to reset your run.

1. When you do have enough journal entries, Allister casts a spell (using the runes from the journal entries) to clear the fog, improving visibility. Then, we can include the maze idea to have the player make their way to the seal.
2. After reaching the seal, the player uses the same spell to undo the seal here.

### Special End Room 01 (Primordial / Fire)

- Narrow rocky path w/ safe zones along the way.
	- We can justify this by saying the Archmage needed spots to rest when making this room?
- Eruptions will occur along the along the path. The player must time their approach.

#### Concerns

… Does the player die if they get hit by an eruption? Or do they get reset to the beginning of the path? Do they get put back at the safe zones?

### Special End Room 02 (Tempest / Wind)

- Large zigzag staircase w/ the seal at the top.
- Player must climb the stairs while lightning traps periodically strike the stairs. The player must avoid getting hit by the lightning.
