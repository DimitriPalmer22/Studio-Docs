## Operation: De-square the game

Here's the main issue with the kit pieces in our game, they are all very basic squares OR you can easily tell that they're a square with a hole cut in them.

We want to increase the *visual complexity* of the levels without adding props. 
- If we remove all the props and just have the kit pieces, how *complex* does the room look?
- Is there anything to break up the flatness of the walls or floors? Any pillars or anything???
