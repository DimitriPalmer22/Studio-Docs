# Wow, These Props Are All for a Very Specific Theme, Aren't They?

- Here's something that's been a problem. Our LDs have kinda been struggling for ideas for their rooms. They've been trying to make each one unique from the rest, but that has quickly been depleting the idea pool.
- Instead, it might be a better idea to have a strong theme for multiple rooms.
	- This way, we can easily reuse props and assets between rooms.
	- Also, we can make sure we exhaust all the ideas for a theme before moving on to the next one.
