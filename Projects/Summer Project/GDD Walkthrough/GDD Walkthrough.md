# [01 - Main Menu](<./01 - Main Menu.excalidraw.png>)

The player opens the game and is greeted by the main menu. From here, they press start to begin the game.

# [02 - Starting Room](<./02 - Starting Room.excalidraw.png>)

The screen starts out completely black and gradually fades in. Allister is in the starting room and sees a *dialogue portal* in the room. You're supposed to go up to it and interact with it to start the dialogue.

Typically, a random one of Allister's allies will talk to Allister during this interaction. During the very first time the player plays the game, though, Magnus will give Allister a run-down of the game's world and mechanics.

Depending on the character that talks to Allister, Allister's base stats will be modified.

After the dialogue interaction is over, the screen fades to black, and a new UI fades in. This is the screen for the Tome Creation process. This is where the player is asked a series of questions that determine their starting tome's stats and spells. The player is then asked a series of questions through this screen. After this is done, the game fades back in. The player can now exit the room.

# [03 - Stats Breakdown](<./03 - Stats Breakdown.excalidraw.png>)

Before we continue, I want to talk about the stats that Allister has. These stats are used to determine how well Allister performs in combat and how much damage he does.

Stats are broken down into 2 categories:
- Character stats
- Elemental stats

Each character (Allister and the enemies) has a set of stats that influences their combat abilities:

Character Stats
- Vitality - Influences maximum HP
- Mana - Influences maximum MP
- Intelligence - Influences how much damage spells do to enemies
- Resilience - Influences how much damage one takes from incoming attacks
- Agility - Influences how fast one moves

Elements in the game:
- None
- Fire
- Ice
- Lightning
- Water
- Earth
- Wind
- Light
- Dark

Resistance:
- For each element, there is a stat that tracks the character's resistance to a certain element. It is a multiplier that reduces damage of that kind.

Attack:
- For each element, there is a stat that tracks the character's affinity for attacks of a certain element. It is a multiplier that increases damage of that kind.

Allister, his tome, and the enemies all have both character stats and elemental stats.

# [04 - Areas](<./04 - Areas.excalidraw.png>)

Ideally, the average run takes about 30 minutes to complete.

The main portion of the game (after the starting room) is divided into 3 areas, each with its own unique aesthetic. There is also a significant increase in difficulty as the player progresses through these areas.
