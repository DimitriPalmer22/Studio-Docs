# Overview of the Journal Entry System

### Journal Entries As Lore Devices
Allister and his allies know very little about Avernoth. The journal entries scattered throughout the game world are a way to provide players with lore and background information that the main characters might not be aware of. 

Through the journal entries, we can provide information about:
- The history of the world during the time Avernoth was initially created
- the Archmage who created Avernoth
- Avernoth itself

### Journal Entries as Progression
We needed some objective for the player to pursue beyond just "get out". It felt too repetitive. Journal entries now have "objectives" the player needs to complete in order to progress the story.
