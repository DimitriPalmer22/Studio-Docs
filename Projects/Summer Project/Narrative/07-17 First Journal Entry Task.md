### Refresher on the "Journal Room" Idea

About 2 weeks ago, we revised the idea for the "journal room". Instead, we decided to go with a desk possibly spawning in each room that the player can interact with to read a journal entry. This is to avoid interrupting gameplay too much.

More about this can be found in the meeting notes here:
[07-01 Narrative Designer Meeting](<../Meeting Notes/07-01 Narrative Designer Meeting.md>)

### The Journal Entries

The GDD briefly describes the journal entries by saying:

> Journal entries are small bits of narrative lore that are delivered to the player. They are written from the perspective of the archmage that created Avernoth. The player can then look back on the journal entries they’ve collected by opening the ***codex***.

The main point of these is to convey to the player as much as we can about the Archmage himself, the Musarun Empire, and the world during the time the Archmage was still alive. Since these were written at different points during the Archmage's creation of Avernoth, we use these to demonstrate the Archmage's further descent into madness.

### The Task

Journal entry #1
- This is the first journal entry that the player will find in Avernoth and is the first journal entry that the Archmage wrote.
- From the Archmage's perspective, write a little about why the Archmage created Avernoth. Something along the lines of "They shouldn't be able to find me here."
- Since the journal entries aren't a necessary part of the gameplay loop, we shouldn't have to worry too much about their length disrupting the flow of gameplay. So, I wanna say we can have closer to like 150 words per entry. In the screenshots below, the word count is about 150 (although, there are a couple of line breaks that take up some space). If necessary though, we can lower the size of the font to allow for more text.

### Future Journal Entry Ideas

Some more possible ideas for future journal entries:
- The Archmage realizes that he failed to fully destroy his research, which opens up the possibility of the Empire using his spells to get into Avernoth
- The actual process of making Avernoth (making the spells and all that)
- The Archmage's history of making spells for the Empire
- A little story about how the Archmage felt when he first learned his spells were being used against the Empire
