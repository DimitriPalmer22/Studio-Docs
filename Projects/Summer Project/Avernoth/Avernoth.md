# Avernoth

This is a folder detailing all the details, mechanics, story beats, and more of [Avernoth](<./Avernoth.md>).

*Avernoth* is an alternate dimension that is the main setting of our game. It is a dark, twisted version of reality that is filled with all sorts of horrors and dangers. The player will explore this world and uncover its secrets as they progress through the game.
