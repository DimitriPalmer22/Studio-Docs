# Avernoth

This is a folder detailing all the details, mechanics, story beats, and more of [Avernoth](<./Avernoth.md>). This note contains a brief synopsis of the game and it's inner workings. 

## Setting

*Avernoth* is an alternate dimension that is the main setting of our game. It is a dark, twisted version of reality that is filled with all sorts of horrors and dangers. The player will explore this world and uncover its secrets as they progress through the game.

## Genre

*Avernoth* is a 3D, third-person, rogue-like action game. The player will explore procedurally generated levels, fight off hordes of enemies, and upgrade their character to through the game. Death means restarting your progress, but characters retain their memories (moving the plot forward).
