# Linear Narrative

In order to make the game's story more compelling, the game's narrative MUST have some sort of linearity.

The progression of the game's narrative is directly tied to the player's collection of the [Journal Entries](<../Journal Entries/Journal Entries.md>).

## Chapters

For our sake, we should divide the game's narrative into several "chapters". Each chapter should have a specific theme and focus on a specific aspect of the game's story. This will allow us to structure the game's narrative in a way that is easy for the player to follow and understand.
