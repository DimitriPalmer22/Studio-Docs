## Events Leading Up to the Game

> These are the events that happen RIGHT before the game starts. They are immediately relevant to the player and should be communicated to the player.

[The Coalition](<./Characters/The Coalition.md>) and [The Musarun Empire](<./Characters/The Musarun Empire.md>) are at war. [Allister](<./Characters/Allister.md>) and [Hidetomo](<./Characters/Hidetomo.md>) are in a heated battle to the death. Hidetomo catches Allister off-guard, strips Allister of his power / tome, and seals Allister in [Avernoth](<../Avernoth.md>).
