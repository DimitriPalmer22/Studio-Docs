# Narrative

These are the events that take place within Avernoth's story.

## LONG Before the Events of the Game

> These are lore elements that are not necessarily relevant to the game, but they provide context and background for the world and its characters. They can be used to flesh out the world and make it feel more alive and immersive.

[The Musarun Empire](<./Characters/The Musarun Empire.md>) has been around since ancient times. They long had a thirst for power and conquest and have always made efforts to expand their influence and control over other lands and peoples.

To aid in their conquest, they enlisted the help of [The Archmage](<./Characters/The Archmage.md>). The Archmage was a brilliant sorcerer who was able to invent the [Tome Altar](<../Mechanics/Tome Altar.md>), a type of magical device used to create [Tomes](<../../Mechanics/Tomes.md>) that under-skilled mages could use to gain powerful abilities (much like giving a regular person a gun). The Archmage's invention quickly bolstered the Musarun Empire's forces, allowing them to conquer more and more lands and peoples.

However, the Archmage soon after realized the weight of his actions. He saw the destruction and chaos that his invention had caused, and he felt guilty for his role in it. This led to a decline in the Archmage's mental state and a rise in his paranoia. In an effort to protect himself from potential threats, the Archmage used his high proficiency in spatial magic to create [Avernoth](<../Avernoth.md>). The Archmage burns his research and flees into Avernoth, hoping to hide from the consequences of his actions. However, while in Avernoth, his mental state slowly decays and is still paranoid that someone or *something* followed him in.

## Events Leading Up to the Game

> These are the events that happen RIGHT before the game starts. They are immediately relevant to the player and should be communicated to the player.

[The Coalition](<./Characters/The Coalition.md>) and [The Musarun Empire](<./Characters/The Musarun Empire.md>) are at war. [Allister](<./Characters/Allister.md>) and [Hidetomo](<./Characters/Hidetomo.md>) are in a heated battle to the death. Hidetomo catches Allister off-guard, strips Allister of his power / tome, and seals Allister in [Avernoth](<../Avernoth.md>).

However, right before Allister is sealed away, [Magnus](<./Characters/Magnus.md>) casts a spell on Allister, Allister and the rest of his allies in [The Coalition](<./Characters/The Coalition.md>) to communicate. Furthermore, a separate spell is cast on Allister, resetting his physical position and state to how it was upon entering [Avernoth](<../Avernoth.md>) whenever he dies.
