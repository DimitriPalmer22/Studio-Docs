## Journal Entry

Journal entries are notes written by [The Archmage](<../Narrative/Characters/The Archmage.md>) while he was spending his time in Avernoth. Each of them is imbued with a **magic rune**.

There are two types of journal entries that can be found in [Avernoth](<../Avernoth.md>).

### Standard Journal Entry

Standard journal entries are just notes that The Archmage wrote while he was in Avernoth. They can be about anything, but they usually contain information about the Archmage's mental state at the time that he wrote the entry. They also divulge more information about the world before the events of the game, as well as the Archmage's thoughts on the world and his situation.

### Research Log

Research logs are journal entries that take note of any **experiments** the Archmage was performing while continuing his research in Avernoth.
