# Tome Altar

Tome altars are magical devices created by [The Archmage](<../Narrative/Characters/The Archmage.md>) to equip low-power mages with useful abilities. They are a key part of the lore and mechanics of *Avernoth*, as they are the main way that players acquire abilities at the start of each run.

## Questions

The Tome Altars don't just give the recipient a [tome](Tome). They present the recipient with a "personality test" that determines the tome they receive. The recipient answers several questions and then the tome is given to them.

However, each time a person takes the assessment, they do not get the same questions every time. This was to prevent too many mages within the [The Musarun Empire](<../Narrative/Characters/The Musarun Empire.md>) from having the same types of abilities, and thus having the same weaknesses.
