
The levels in our game are themed around a magical library, not unlike Raya Lucaria in Elden Ring. The library is filled with books, scrolls, and other magical artifacts. The levels are meant to be closed spaces, with only open areas in the center of the level.

### Props for Filling Empty Space

One challenge of designing these levels is that often times, parts of the level will feel too open and boring. Medium / Large, obstructive props do a good job of filling up the space and do well to draw away from the emptiness of the playable area. However, sometimes we need smaller props to fill in the gaps without obstructing the player's view of the level.

- An extremely large pile of books (like the ones in Elden Ring)
- A single statue
	- Broken
	- Intact
- A single bookshelf
- A single table
- A single chair
- A single bench

### Unobstructive Props for Filling Playable Area

Another issue is that the playable area of these levels will feel so large and empty that it becomes painfully distracting to the player. Small and unobstructive props can litter the playable area to make it feel more alive and less empty without obstructing the player's view / movement of the level.

#### Small Props
- Small plants
- Scrolls
	- A littered pile of scrolls
	- An individual scroll
- Small books
	- A littered pile of books
	- An individual book
- Candles
	- A single candle
	- A group of candles
- A single lantern
- A single rug
- A single tapestry
- A single painting

#### Debris
- Broken pieces of wood (Where did this wood come from? A table? a Chair? A bookshelf?)
- Broken pieces of stone (Where did this stone come from? A wall? A pillar?)
- Broken candles
