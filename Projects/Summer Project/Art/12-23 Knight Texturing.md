There are three variations of the knight enemy in the game. We'll use color to make each one visually distinct from the others. This way, the player can immediately recognize the type of enemy they are about to fight. 

Each enemy is elementally themed, so that should influence the color palette used to texture them. However, to keep their designs interesting, they should NOT be fully monochromatic. 

### Primordial (Fire) Knight
- This enemy uses the same fire spear you made a while ago
- The enemy's texture should incorporate heavy red accents to reflect its fire element


### Twilight (Dark) Knight
- This enemy uses a dark-themed sword
- The enemy's texture should incorporate deep purple (and mayblack) accents to reflect its dark element
