# Wisps

Wisps (name pending) are little magical beings that have various effects tied to them depending on their type.
- They can be summoned through various means (spells / addendums / etc.)
- Some of them are temporary, while others are permanent until dismissed.
- They can be used for combat or utility purposes.

## Why Even Make This a Mechanic

I need more ways to introduce variety and interesting mechanics into the game, and wisps can provide that.
