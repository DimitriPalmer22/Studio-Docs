I added support for a whole bunch of different sounds in the game. Right now, everything is using placeholders, but the placeholders can be easily replaced.
### Sound Effects For
- Taking damage
- Dying (Both for the enemies and the player)

- "Creating" a spell (i.e. spawning a fireball)
- Firing a spell (i.e. releasing a fireball)
- a subtle sound effect for when the player is able to press the attack button again to continue their combo

- When you enter a room (when the player first spawns in)
- When a room starts (when the enemies first spawn in and the player gains control of Allister)
- Clearing a room (when all the enemies are killed)

- Text being written in the dialogue
- Going to the next line in the dialogue

- Mana essence being collected
