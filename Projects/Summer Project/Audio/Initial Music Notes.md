# Music Notes

### Where Can Different Music Tracks Play?

- The reginning room
- Shop room
- Upgrade room
- In the middle of combat
	- Normal combat rooms
	- The larger, more intense combat rooms at the end of each floor
- During dialogue
- Main menu

### Genre

#### Overall Music Genre

I'm thinking the game's overall can be described as "Ambient Jungle".

#### Description of the Genre
- Atmospheric and airy soundscapes with lush and large reverbs & delays. This should should be like the main thing that makes the game feel magical.
- The whole track should be bitcrushed to give it a more retro feel. You can probably run the whole track through a vintage sampler plugin to get this effect.
- The energy of the track should match the energy of the current situation.
	- For example, if the player is in a combat situation, the drums should be a lot more present and the track should be more energetic.
	- If the player is currently in dialogue or something, the track should be more subdued and atmospheric.
- a subtle 808 bass (not the kind found in modern trap instrumentals, but closer to the kind you would find on an original 808 drum machine)
- Typically, jungle tracks have a lot of sampled elements to fill up the track.
	- Things like rain or birds chirping quietly in the background could be good examples of this.
- There aren't too many synthesized elements in jungle music, but if you do use them, they should be very subtle and airy.
	- If anything, you could record a synthesized sound, export it, and resample it to give it that retro feel. You usually get a much warmer and more retro sound if you record the sound at a higher pitch (possibly at a lower bitrate too), then resample it and pitch it back down to the desired pitch.

- Track 3: State of Emergence <https://youtu.be/4OT8ZJ6kJ74?si=uR2E6_02YbhHKZfW&t=991>
- Track 4: Veil of Mist <https://youtu.be/4OT8ZJ6kJ74?si=4JFBrePdv5_v2X3M&t=1509>
- Track 6: Wanderer <https://youtu.be/4OT8ZJ6kJ74?si=1klZMDD84-YJtXsr&t=2423>
- Track 7: Nirmala II <https://youtu.be/4OT8ZJ6kJ74?si=skMmX6SSZq2rNTj6&t=2931>

I think these are pretty good refs for an ambient jungle type of sound, but they're a little too bright and aren't very bitcrushed.

##### Source Direct - Secret Liaison

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=480Q_aZUcxw>

I like this track but it's really drum-heavy. This could work for when the player is in a combat situation, but it probably won't fit as much after the player has cleared a room or is talking to one of the other characters.

##### Seba - So Long

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MhcqYxogdsU>

 Parts of this song are really nice for the slower sections of the game (minimal drums, not too much going on). You see this specifically about 6 minutes in.

##### The Drumkilla - Deep Forest

<https://soundcloud.com/the-drumkilla/deep-forest>

This is another example of a high-energy track. The drums & 808 pattern might be a little too intense for normal combat, though. Maybe something like this could play for the more intense combat rooms at the end of each floor.
